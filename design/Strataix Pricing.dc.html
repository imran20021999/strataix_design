FAQ

Common questions, in plain words.

Do you resell provider tokens?
No. You bring your own provider keys. We pass traffic through at the provider's list price, and bill a flat platform fee. You keep your existing enterprise discounts.
What counts as a "routed request"?
One request from your app to our gateway. Fallback retries and cache hits do not count. Internal health checks don't count.
Can we start on Growth annual and change our mind?
Yes. Change or cancel any time; we prorate to the day and refund the unused balance to your card or next invoice.
Is my data used to train models?
Never. Your prompts are sent to your providers under their data policy. We retain logs only for the window you set (7–365 days) and you can redact PII at the gateway before anything is logged.
Do you offer a discount for startups or OSS?
Yes · Growth free for 12 months for early-stage startups (under $3M raised) and recognized OSS projects. Apply →
